Certify Int lemmas of the nonlinear solver by polynomial refutations

Pull request levnach/z3#134 into master · State: OPEN (not merged)

Change commit: 55a163ae6 · Date: 2026-11-13T18:54:13Z · Loop iteration 133

In one paragraph

This PR certifies clauses that are true because of what a product of terms of sort Int means. One such clause says that m ≥ 2 and n ≥ 3 give m·n ≥ 6. Z3's solver for nonlinear arithmetic uses such clauses as theory lemmas. Until now a refutation that needed one was rejected, or it failed in Lean. The pipeline now searches for a polynomial refutation of the lemma's literals, and Lean proves the lemma from it. Nothing new is trusted: no axiom is added, and Lean checks the polynomial identity itself. On the nonlinear test corpus, 24 of 78 unsat queries now verify in Lean, up from 14.

Background

Since #133 a product of Int terms is an atom of linear arithmetic, so refutations that use only a product's bounds were already certified. A refutation that used a lemma of the nonlinear solver was still rejected by name. The same reasoning also reached the clause log under other hints, such as cnf and cut, and Lean's tactics could not prove those clauses. The work serves milestone 4 of the plan, “Broader theories and integration”, which names nonlinear arithmetic.

What changed

A polynomial refutation works like a receipt: anyone can add up the lines and see that the total is impossible.

  • Search. A new function, polynomial_refutation, finds multipliers for the facts a clause denies, their pairwise products, and squares, so that the sum is −1.
  • Exporter. A lemma of the nonlinear solver with such a refutation now enters the certificate under the hint nla, and so does a clause the log merely assumes on that solver's word.
  • Exporter. When trimming removed unit clauses that a lemma needs, the untrimmed log is replayed, with a warning.
  • Reconstructor. The Lean proof is written from the refutation, like a Farkas lemma proof with products among the summands.
  • Tests and docs. A new test module covers the search, 18 documented inputs, pinned logs, and tampered certificates.

The same proof is tried for cut, bound, and cnf lemmas over Int products. A solver lemma without a refutation is still rejected. No C++ file changed.

Review

The reviewer approved, with no blocker and no major finding.

  • Minor, fixed: the gain was invisible to another harness. The reviewer ran the new inputs through z3test's harness, which trims the log and builds the certificate in two calls. Four of the ten newly certified corpus queries were not certified there. The fallback to the untrimmed log moved into build_certificate, which that harness calls too.
  • Minor, documented, not changed: five rejections became Lean failures. Five inputs used to be rejected by the exporter with a clear message. Their solver lemmas now have refutations, so they are exported and fail in Lean on another step. Nothing unsound is accepted, but the gap shows up later. No rule was found that rejects them early without also rejecting inputs that verify, so the docs now name them.
  • Minor, fixed: a test that could not fail. A test named “stays rejected” never reached its rejection branch. It now pins each input's hints and number of polynomial proofs, and a new test asserts that Lean rejects both inputs.
  • Four nits and one suggestion, all applied. A comment and the docs were corrected, and a blank line was removed. The second replay now shares the first one's deadline and warns. A cut or cnf lemma keeps its older proofs when the search raises an error.

Risks and open questions

  • A harness that counts rejections and Lean failures separately will see the five inputs above move.
  • The search runs in both the exporter and the reconstructor, which could disagree at the size limit. This was not observed.
  • A search that hits its resource limit reads as “no refutation”. Its cost on large nonlinear logs was not measured.
  • Three random seeds seem to verify because certificates became more compact, not through a polynomial proof. This was not analysed further.
  • The final full test run took almost twice as long as earlier runs. The cause was not measured.

What comes next

  • Lemmas that conclude an equality, equalities stated as two bounds, and squares of sums.
  • The rewriter's sign split, behind most random seeds that still fail in Lean.
  • Products of Real terms, the largest rejected group.
  • Reading Z3's own refutation from the hint, which needs a C++ change.

Glossary

assertion
A formula the input states as true, written (assert ...) in SMT-LIB.
atom
A piece that a theory treats as one indivisible unit. In linear arithmetic over 3x + y, the atoms are x and y.
axiom
A statement accepted without proof. A certificate that adds one is only as reliable as that statement.
bound
A comparison of a term with a constant, such as m ≥ 2. Also the name of a hint for a lemma that derives one.
certificate
A file recording the steps of a refutation so that another program can check them.
clause
A disjunction of literals, such as “not A, or B, or not C”.
clause log
The list of clauses a solver wrote while it searched, each with a note on where it came from.
cnf
Conjunctive normal form: a formula written as a conjunction of clauses. As a hint, it marks a clause that ties an assertion to that form.
cut
A lemma of integer arithmetic that removes non-integer solutions. From 2x ≥ 1 over the integers it follows that x ≥ 1.
Farkas lemma
The fact that inconsistent linear inequalities can be added up, with nonnegative multipliers, to an obvious falsehood such as 0 ≤ -1.
hint
A label on a logged clause naming the kind of reasoning that produced it, for example nla or cut.
linear arithmetic
Arithmetic where variables are only added and multiplied by constants, as in 2x + 3y ≤ 7.
literal
An atomic formula or its negation, such as m ≥ 2 or not (m ≥ 2).
multiplier
The constant by which a fact is scaled before facts are added together.
nonlinear arithmetic
Arithmetic where variables are multiplied with each other, as in m * n < 6.
polynomial
A sum of products of variables and constants, such as m*n - 3m + 6.
polynomial refutation
Evidence that some facts cannot all hold. Nonnegative multiples of the facts, of products of two facts, and of squares add up to a negative constant.
product
The result of multiplying terms, called its factors. In (* m n) the factors are m and n.
query
One (check-sat) call in an SMT-LIB file, together with the assertions in force at that point.
refutation
A proof that a set of assertions leads to a contradiction.
sorry
Lean's placeholder for a missing proof. A file that uses it is not a complete proof.
sort
The type of a value, such as Int, Real, or Bool.
tactic
A Lean command that tries to build a proof automatically. omega and grind are tactics.
term
An expression that denotes a value, such as m, (+ m 1), or (* m n).
theory lemma
A clause that is true by the rules of a theory alone, such as arithmetic, whatever the input says.
trimming
Removing from a clause log the clauses the refutation does not use.
unit propagation
When all literals of a clause but one are known false, the last one must be true. A unit clause has a single literal.
